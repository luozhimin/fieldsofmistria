Adeline Romance Guide - Fields of Mistria

Adeline is one of the 12 marriage candidates in Fields of Mistria. Birthday: Winter 18. Acting town leader of Mistria and one of the noble siblings.

About Adeline

Adeline is the organized, direct and friendly acting leader of Mistria — the sister of Eiland, responsible for the town's restoration projects. Her romance arc is all about helping her finally relax and put herself first for once.

How to Romance Adeline

  • Talk to her every day — repeated conversations raise friendship fast.
  • Give loved gifts, especially on the birthday (Winter 18), for a big friendship spike.
  • Heart events fire at the 2, 4, 6 and 8 heart marks.
  • Reach 10 hearts, craft the engagement ring, and propose to get married.
Tulip (plant in spring), Heather (forage in fall), Snapdragon (forage in winter). The easiest birthday gift is Coffee — buy it from Darcy's Saturday Market stall for 100 Tesserae.
