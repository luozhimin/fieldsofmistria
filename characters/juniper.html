Juniper Romance Guide - Fields of Mistria

Juniper is one of the 12 marriage candidates in Fields of Mistria. Birthday: Fall 26. Sorceress and bathhouse owner with her familiar Dozy.

About Juniper

Juniper is the sarcastic, confident sorceress who runs the Bathhouse. She loves magical ingredients and oddities — and has a soft spot for pizza. Her interactions with Valen are some of the town's best.

How to Romance Juniper

  • Talk to her every day — repeated conversations raise friendship fast.
  • Give loved gifts, especially on the birthday (Fall 26), for a big friendship spike.
  • Heart events fire at the 2, 4, 6 and 8 heart marks.
  • Reach 10 hearts, craft the engagement ring, and propose to get married.
Red Toadstools and Monster Powder drop from early mine enemies — the fastest reliable gifts.
