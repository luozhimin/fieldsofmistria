Reina Romance Guide - Fields of Mistria

Reina is one of the 12 marriage candidates in Fields of Mistria. Birthday: Fall 12. Head cook at the Sleeping Dragon Inn.

About Reina

Reina is the cheerful, energetic head cook at the Sleeping Dragon Inn. She loves reading and making food, and her taste leans toward finished dishes — so cooking skill pays off directly.

How to Romance Reina

  • Talk to her every day — repeated conversations raise friendship fast.
  • Give loved gifts, especially on the birthday (Fall 12), for a big friendship spike.
  • Heart events fire at the 2, 4, 6 and 8 heart marks.
  • Reach 10 hearts, craft the engagement ring, and propose to get married.
Cheese, flour, rice and sugar are solid liked gifts early; Cabbage Slaw is a great cooked option.
