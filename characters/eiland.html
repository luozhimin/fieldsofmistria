Eiland Romance Guide - Fields of Mistria

Eiland is one of the 12 marriage candidates in Fields of Mistria. Birthday: Summer 20. Archaeologist and head of the Historical Society, Adeline's brother.

About Eiland

Eiland is the friendly, outgoing noble archaeologist who hunts for the lost pieces of an ancient set of armor. He has a serious sweet tooth — most of his loved gifts are desserts.

How to Romance Eiland

  • Talk to him every day — repeated conversations raise friendship fast.
  • Give loved gifts, especially on the birthday (Summer 20), for a big friendship spike.
  • Heart events fire at the 2, 4, 6 and 8 heart marks.
  • Reach 10 hearts, craft the engagement ring, and propose to get married.
Caramel Candy (sugar + butter) is a fast fallback; Strawberry Shortcake is his ultimate favorite.
