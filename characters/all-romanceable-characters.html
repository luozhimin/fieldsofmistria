Who Can You Actually Marry?

All 12 candidates can be romanced, proposed to and married in the 1.0 release. Two are special:

  • Caldarus and Seridia are story-gated — you must progress the main mines storyline before their romance paths open up.
  • The other 10 can be met through ordinary town exploration from early game.
Not every single-looking villager is romanceable. The most common misconception is Olric — March's brother is friendly and giftable, but he is not one of the 12 marriage candidates (as of the current release).

How Romance Works

  1. Talk to your candidate every day and give liked or loved gifts to raise hearts.
  2. Heart events trigger every two hearts — watch your mailbox for letters.
  3. Reach 10 hearts to unlock the engagement ring recipe (sent by Elsie).
  4. Craft the ring, propose, and plan your wedding.

Full step-by-step details in our How to Get Married guide, plus the Gift Guide for loved gifts.
