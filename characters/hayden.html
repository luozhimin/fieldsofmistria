Hayden Romance Guide - Fields of Mistria

Hayden is one of the 12 marriage candidates in Fields of Mistria. Birthday: Winter 8. Sweetwater Farm owner with a pet chicken named Henrietta.

About Hayden

Hayden is the earnest, friendly owner of Sweetwater Farm and an animal specialist. His storyline nods to being a 'single dad' of sorts raising his beloved chicken Henrietta.

How to Romance Hayden

  • Talk to him every day — repeated conversations raise friendship fast.
  • Give loved gifts, especially on the birthday (Winter 8), for a big friendship spike.
  • Heart events fire at the 2, 4, 6 and 8 heart marks.
  • Reach 10 hearts, craft the engagement ring, and propose to get married.
Eggs, milk, butter and cheese are reliable liked gifts year-round. The best birthday gift is a Golden Egg or Golden Milk.
