Valen Romance Guide - Fields of Mistria

Valen is one of the 12 marriage candidates in Fields of Mistria. Birthday: Fall 1. Town doctor with a passion for alchemy.

About Valen

Valen is Mistria's calm, reserved doctor who is deeply caring and passionate about alchemy and medical research. Beneath her professional exterior hides a wonderfully sarcastic sense of humor.

How to Romance Valen

  • Talk to her every day — repeated conversations raise friendship fast.
  • Give loved gifts, especially on the birthday (Fall 1), for a big friendship spike.
  • Heart events fire at the 2, 4, 6 and 8 heart marks.
  • Reach 10 hearts, craft the engagement ring, and propose to get married.
Dandelions (forage in spring) and seaweed (ocean dives) are good early picks; Deep Sea Soup is her signature dish.
