Balor Romance Guide - Fields of Mistria

Balor is one of the 12 marriage candidates in Fields of Mistria. Birthday: Summer 10. Traveling merchant who brings rare goods into Mistria.

About Balor

Balor is the confident, flirtatious traveling merchant who collects and ships your goods. His storyline is about his self-serving instincts versus his growing loyalty to Mistria.

How to Romance Balor

  • Talk to him every day — repeated conversations raise friendship fast.
  • Give loved gifts, especially on the birthday (Summer 10), for a big friendship spike.
  • Heart events fire at the 2, 4, 6 and 8 heart marks.
  • Reach 10 hearts, craft the engagement ring, and propose to get married.
Ruby — mine ruby nodes in the upper levels (floors 1-20). A regular ruby is a fine birthday gift in year one.
