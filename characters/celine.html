About Celine

Celine is one of the 12 romance and marriage candidates introduced in Fields of Mistria. Like every romance candidate, she has her own schedule, loved gifts and heart events.

How to Romance Celine

The romance system works the same for every candidate:

  • Talk to Celine every day — repeated conversations raise friendship faster than the genre norm.
  • Give loved gifts, especially on her birthday, for a big friendship spike.
  • Heart events fire at the 2, 4, 6 and 8 heart marks.
  • Reach 10 hearts, craft the engagement ring, and propose to get married.
Friendship pays off whether or not you plan to marry — higher hearts unlock recipes, shop discounts and story beats for every villager.
