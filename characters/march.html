About March

March is one of the 12 romance and marriage candidates in Fields of Mistria. He's a blacksmith, and like every candidate, has his own schedule, loved gifts and heart events.

How to Romance March

  • Talk to March every day — repeated conversations raise friendship faster than the genre norm.
  • Give loved gifts, especially on his birthday, for a big friendship spike.
  • Heart events fire at the 2, 4, 6 and 8 heart marks.
  • Reach 10 hearts, craft the engagement ring, and propose to get married.
March runs the forge on the northwest edge of town — the same anvil where you upgrade your tools.
