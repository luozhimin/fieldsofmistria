Best Crops & How to Make Money in Fields of Mistria

Early income is tight — here's how to earn Tesserae fast.

Early Money Routes

  • Fishing — before the mines open, fishing offers a simple, steady income.
  • Mining — once mining is available, craft tools or armor at the forge and sell the extras.
  • Quest board — daily tasks pay money, tools and seeds, and are your most reliable early source.

Best Crops Per Season

Buy whichever crops you can afford at the start of each season, but look for crops that cost little and sell for noticeably more. If you can wait, prioritize crops that regrow every few days over one-time harvests.

Seed Maker & Late-Game Profit

Unlocking the seed maker lets you turn regrowing crops into sellable seeds for extra profit. Later on, ornamental crop items unlocked through the Caldarus perk become one of the best money makers in the game.

A good rule of thumb: sell crafted or mined goods, and stash overflow in your farmhouse chest instead of panic-selling when your bag fills.
