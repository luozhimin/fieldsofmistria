How to Have Children in Fields of Mistria

Children are optional and only come up once you're married.

The Mystical Feather

Children are optional and only come up once you're married. A while into married life, a cutscene gives you a Mystical Feather. Using it opens a family planning conversation whenever you feel ready.

The Parent Path

Starting a family is tied to the Mystical Feather and dreams of the Great Bird. It's an optional path — you can remain child-free if you prefer, with no gameplay penalty.

Make sure you've completed marriage first — the children system only becomes available after you settle into married life.
