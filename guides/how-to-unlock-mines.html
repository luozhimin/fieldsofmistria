How to Unlock the Mines in Fields of Mistria

The mines gate magic, late-game ores and most of the combat skill tree. Here's how to open them.

Why the Mines Matter

The mines are the only reliable source of the ore and gems you need to upgrade your tools and gear. Opening them also unlocks the magic system, which gives you spells that water crops and restore stamina. If you're stuck at the start, the mines are usually the first wall.

Prerequisite: Reach Town Rank Stone

The mines are blocked by the Town Rank system. You need to reach Town Rank Stone (level 10) before the quest chain opens. Raise your renown by:

  • Completing the daily quest board tasks
  • Donating items to the museum
  • Building friendship with villagers
Renown is your main progress wall early on. Work the request board every day — quest rewards give money, tools and even free seeds, which helps more than farming alone in the first weeks.

The Mines Layout

Once open, the mines run deep and are split into four biomes. An elevator activates every five floors, so you can leave and return without redoing the climb.

BiomeFloorsNotes
Upper Mines1 – 19First floor has no monsters.
Tide Caverns21 – 39Unlocked after the floor 20 offering.
Deep Earth41 – 59Harder enemies, more loot.
Lava Caves61 – 79Endgame biome.

How to Descend

You find ladders hidden inside rocks and inside the monsters you defeat. Bring food to refill stamina and watch the clock so you don't pass out underground.

Next Step: Unlock Magic

Reaching floor 10 and then going home to sleep triggers Caldarus to appear and gift you your first spell, Full Restore. From there, continue to unlock more spells at floors 20 and 40.
