How to Unlock Mounts in Fields of Mistria

Mounts arrive with the 1.0 update — here's how to get your first ride.

Unlocking Your Mount

Your first mount is the Big Chicken, unlocked after upgrading your barn. Once the barn is upgraded, you can unlock the mount plus additional mount skins.

Mount Tips

  • Rebind your mount key to a comfortable button (many players prefer F) so you can call it quickly.
  • Mounts speed up travel around the map and make daily routes faster.
Mounts are a quality-of-life unlock tied to ranching. If you haven't started ranching yet, visit Ryis's shop for a coop or barn blueprint, then adopt animals from Hayden at Sweetwater Farm.
