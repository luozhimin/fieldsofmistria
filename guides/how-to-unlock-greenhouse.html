How to Unlock the Greenhouse in Fields of Mistria

The greenhouse lets you grow crops year-round — here's what it takes.

Requirements

The greenhouse follows the Upgrade Carpenter quest and requires Town Rank 70. You'll also need to gather:

  • 100 Refined Stone
  • 100 Glass
  • 30 Crystal
  • 30 Obsidian

Why It's Worth It

Growing out-of-season crops removes the biggest seasonal constraint on your income. It's a long-term goal, so stockpile materials as you go rather than grinding everything at once.

This is a mid-to-late game unlock. Focus on the mines and tool upgrades first.
