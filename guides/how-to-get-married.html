How to Get Married in Fields of Mistria

Marriage arrived with the 1.0 update. Here's the full path to the altar.

Reach 10 Hearts

Since the 1.0 release, you can reach Heart Level 10 with a romance candidate. Raise hearts through the 2, 4, 6 and 8 heart events by talking to them daily and giving loved gifts.

Craft the Engagement Ring

Once at 10 hearts, craft the Engagement Ring from Elsie's recipe at the forge. The final ingredient comes from deep in the mines (floor 100).

Propose & Hold the Wedding

With the ring in hand, propose to your partner. You can then hold a fully customizable wedding, complete with spouse routines after you settle into married life.

The 12 Marriage Candidates

Adeline, Balor, Caldarus, Celine, Eiland, Hayden, Juniper, March, Reina, Ryis, Seridia and Valen. See the Characters section for individual guides.
