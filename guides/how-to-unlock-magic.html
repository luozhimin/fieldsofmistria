How to Unlock Magic & Get More Spells in Fields of Mistria

Magic unlocks as you descend the mines. Each spell is tied to a specific floor milestone.

How to Unlock Magic

After you open the mines, make your way down to floor 10 by smashing rocks with your pickaxe until you find each set of ladders. Once you reach floor 10, head back home and go to sleep. Caldarus will appear and bestow your first spell.

Floor 20 Offering: Summon Rain

At floor 20 you'll find a tablet and a door blocking your way. Speak to Eiland, then Juniper, who translates the tablet and tells you what offerings to place in the four clawed hands.

Required Offerings

  • A Lantern Moth
  • A Ruby
  • A Stone Loach
  • An Upper Mines Mushroom

All four can be found in the first 20 floors of the mine (the Upper Mines). Place them as offerings, and the door opens. Caldarus then grants you Summon Rain.

Floor 40 Offering: Growth

A similar door appears every 20 floors. Opening the floor 40 door grants your third spell, Growth, which fully grows crops in a 3x3 area and can progress tree growth by one stage.

The 1.0 release makes the full mines storyline completable — push deeper to break the final Fire Seal and free Caldarus to unlock the last elemental spell.
