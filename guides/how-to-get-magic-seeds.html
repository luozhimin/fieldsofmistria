How to Get Magic Seeds in Fields of Mistria

A mystery seed that grows into a random seasonal reward — here's how to unlock it, plant it, and what it can grow.

How to Get Magic Seeds

Magic Seeds are not sold in any shop. They only appear as a rare drop when you harvest crops, and only after you unlock the Earthly Essence farming perk. Spend Farming Essence on this perk at the dragon shrine (Caldarus statue) near your house — once it's active, every harvested crop has a low chance of dropping a Magic Seed.

Maximize your Magic Seed odds by harvesting high-value, high-volume crops. The more harvests you complete each season, the more rolls you get at the drop table.

How to Plant & Grow Them

  • Season: Magic Seeds can be planted in all seasons.
  • Growth time: About 5 days to maturity.
  • Harvest: The plant is harvested once — after that it's gone.
  • Fast-forward: Cast the Growth spell to instantly mature the plant, then harvest immediately.

What Magic Seeds Grow (By Season)

The harvest is random and depends on the current season. You always get exactly one of the following:

Tips & Related Skills

  • Green Thumb — planting may auto-water the tile.
  • Superb Sower — charge seeds to plant a wider area.
  • Refreshing — watering can refund stamina.
  • Well Watered — watered crops may keep their water overnight.
The Growth spell is your best friend here — unlock it at mine floor 40, then use it to skip the 5-day wait on every Magic Seed you plant.
